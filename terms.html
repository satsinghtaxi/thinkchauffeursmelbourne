Terms & conditions

Last updated 1 January 2026. These terms apply to every booking made with Think Chauffeurs Melbourne.

1. Bookings

A booking is confirmed only when we send you a written confirmation containing the pickup time, vehicle class and quoted price. Requests submitted by form or email are not confirmed until you receive that message.

2. Pricing

Quoted prices are fixed and inclusive of tolls, airport parking, GST, meet-and-greet and the standard waiting time set out below. Additional stops, route changes requested on the day, extended waiting, and after-hours or public holiday surcharges are charged only where disclosed to you in advance or agreed at the time.

3. Waiting time

Domestic airport pickups include 30 minutes of complimentary waiting from landing; international pickups include 60 minutes from landing. All other pickups include 10 minutes. Waiting beyond these allowances is charged in 15-minute increments at the applicable hourly rate.

4. Cancellations

Cancellations made more than 12 hours before the scheduled pickup are free of charge. Cancellations inside 12 hours incur 50% of the quoted fare. No-shows are charged in full. Weddings, tours and multi-vehicle bookings may carry separate terms, which are stated in your quote. Flight cancellations outside your control are assessed case by case.

5. Passengers and luggage

Passenger and luggage capacities stated for each vehicle class are maximums and cannot be exceeded for safety and insurance reasons. If actual numbers exceed those advised at booking, we may need to supply an additional vehicle at extra cost or decline carriage.

6. Child restraints

Australian-standard capsules, child seats and boosters are supplied and fitted at no charge when requested at the time of booking. We cannot carry children without an appropriate restraint.

7. Conduct and damage

All vehicles are strictly non-smoking, including vaping. Passengers are responsible for the cost of cleaning or repairing any damage or soiling caused during their journey. Chauffeurs may end a journey where passenger behaviour is unsafe, unlawful or abusive.

8. Delays and liability

We plan routes with generous buffers and track flights, but we are not liable for losses arising from traffic incidents, extreme weather, road closures or other events beyond our reasonable control. Nothing in these terms excludes rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law.

9. Payment

Payment is due on completion of the journey unless you hold an approved corporate account, in which case invoices are payable within 14 days. A tax invoice is issued for every trip.

10. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia.
