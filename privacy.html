Privacy policy

Last updated 1 January 2026. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you submit a booking or enquiry we collect your name, phone number, email address, trip details and any notes you choose to provide (such as a flight number or child-seat requirement). We collect this only to quote, confirm and deliver your transfer.

How your enquiry is handled

Our forms are processed by Formspree, which forwards the submission to our booking inbox. Your details are transmitted over an encrypted connection. We do not run any database of website visitors and we do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

How long we keep it

Booking records are retained for seven years to satisfy Australian tax and record-keeping obligations. Unsuccessful enquiries are deleted from our inbox within 12 months.

Who we share it with

Only the chauffeur assigned to your booking, and only the details needed to complete it. Payment is handled by our payment provider — we do not store full card numbers on our systems.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising or tracking cookies. If analytics are enabled in future, this policy will be updated and disclosed before any change takes effect.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing book@thinkchauffeursmelbourne.com.au. We respond to all requests within 30 days.

Complaints

If you believe we have mishandled your information, contact us first so we can put it right. You may also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
