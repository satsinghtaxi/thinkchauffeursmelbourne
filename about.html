Our story

Eighteen years on Melbourne roads

Think Chauffeurs began with one sedan and a simple frustration: transfers that were quoted one way and invoiced another, with cars that turned up late and drivers who treated the job as temporary. We built the opposite.

Today we run a fleet of late-model sedans, SUVs and people movers with a team of career chauffeurs — not casual gig drivers. Every price is fixed in writing before you travel, every airport job is flight-tracked, and every booking has a backup vehicle assigned behind it.

We are trusted by Collins Street law firms, visiting executives, wedding planners and families who simply want the airport run handled properly.
