Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How far in advance should I book?
We recommend 24 hours for standard transfers and 5–7 days for weddings, events and full-day tours. Same-day bookings are welcome subject to availability — call us and we will confirm within minutes.
Do you track flights?
Yes. Every airport booking is monitored against live flight data, so your chauffeur adjusts automatically for early or delayed arrivals. Domestic arrivals include 30 minutes of complimentary waiting time; international arrivals include 60 minutes.
Is the price really fixed?
Yes. Your written quote is all-inclusive — tolls, airport parking, GST, meet-and-greet and standard waiting time. There is no surge pricing and no meter. Extra stops or extended waiting are only ever charged with your prior agreement.
Can you supply baby seats and boosters?
Yes — Australian-standard capsules, baby seats and boosters are supplied and fitted at no extra charge. Please tell us the ages of your children at the time of booking.
What is your cancellation policy?
Cancel more than 12 hours before pickup and there is no charge. Inside 12 hours a 50% fee applies, and no-shows are charged in full. Flight cancellations outside your control are always handled case by case.
How do I pay?
All major credit cards, bank transfer, or a corporate account with monthly invoicing. A tax invoice is issued for every trip.
